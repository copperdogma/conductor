# Thinking-level calibration

Use effort as a task-level decision variable, with the same privacy, owner,
scope and spend boundaries as the parent campaign. Provider documentation is
guidance; local paired evidence decides. More thinking is neither automatically
better nor automatically worth its cost.

## Plan before spending

Record one compact plan per task:

- exact candidate route and supported controls, default, wire parameter and
  evidence that it is honored; incumbent and judge settings separately
- starting setting and task-specific hypothesis, adequate total output allowance
- conditional lower/higher settings and source-backed decision triggers
- frozen calibration cases/strata, metrics, meaningful benefit threshold,
  maximum additional arms/calls and their inclusive reservations
- exit conditions, confirmation material and what remains unmeasured

Normally use the starting arm plus at most two justified semantic calibration
alternatives, consistent with the owner protocol. An adaptive control may need a supported budget rather
than a named level. Do not assume `none`, a forced tool or a sampling setting is
supported. Verify interactions with tools, modality and schema. Distinguish
reasoning level from image detail, output allowance, service tier and model ID.
Include hidden thinking, controls, independent judging and recovery in the cap.
Allow routine operational repair without consuming an arm intended for semantic
calibration, but record its configuration and finite recovery-call reservation
separately. Disclose an output/effort change that can alter the answer; repair
does not license retuning a complete valid quality failure.

## Choose the next experiment from evidence

**Higher effort:** plausible when valid answers miss a valuable correction,
attribution, ambiguity, source-grounding or completeness requirement and measured
cost/time leaves room for a better answer. Choose the smallest informative
stronger setting; maximum is appropriate when the question is the capability
ceiling and that cost is justified. Vendor claims alone do not establish payoff.
After a failure, stop that arm and classify the defect against source truth.
A preapproved stronger arm may still test the same frozen discriminating slice,
with companion positive/negative or safety controls chosen before its outputs.
Preserve the first failure; do not retry the same setting until it passes or
keep escalating until a winner appears.

**Lower effort:** plausible when quality passes but thinking dominates measured
cost/latency, or a faster/cheaper answer could change adoption. Preserve all
quality/safety gates; reducing effort may regress uncertainty or provenance.
An input-dominated bill provides little cost-saving headroom: estimate the bound
from actual token accounting rather than a nominal level name. Quality value
can justify a test even without an economics improvement.

**No further level:** warranted when the task cannot tolerate even a defensible
minimum cost/time, inputs/privacy/route contract are ineligible, the evaluator
cannot distinguish quality, or no plausible valuable gain remains. Give the
specific source/usage/gate evidence. A cheap incumbent or one easy perfect case
alone is insufficient. Pure access/adapter errors call for transport recovery,
not a paid effort sweep; truncation first calls for adequate supported output
headroom. Never count untested levels as failures.

If observations reveal an unplanned but useful level, write its hypothesis,
cases, metrics, arm limit and reservations **before** calling it. Proceed without
another approval only when the selected proposal explicitly allows such bounded
adaptive calibration and this arm fits its constraints. Remaining dollars alone
are not authorization. Otherwise prepare the concrete additional selection;
closed attempts remain immutable evidence, not reusable spending permission.

## Compare fairly and finish with a decision

Keep source bytes, prompt, schema, scorer/golden, route, service tier and
sampling fixed when isolating effort. Use one adequate total-output cap across
candidate effort arms where supported; record incumbent production allowance
separately. If output allowance or another content-affecting control must change,
label a combined configuration comparison rather than attributing its effect
solely to thinking. Exact subject/provider identity, cache keys and request
grouping remain qualified for every materially changed arm.

Use paired cases and fresh same-input maintained controls for relative claims;
reuse a frozen control only with documented unchanged inputs/configuration and
comparable timing conditions. Label cached/reused controls and timing limits.
Keep judge identity, settings and rubric fixed and blind to arm labels. Rejudge
saved outputs only for a demonstrated review defect. Compare correctness,
coverage and uncertainty as well as billed thinking, total task cost, inference
latency, failures and overhead. Never equate reported zero thinking with proof
of disabled reasoning, or model self-confidence with a safe routing rule.

Results used to choose effort are exposed calibration, including a re-tested
failure. Freeze the selected setting before confirmation on held-out or
prospectively reserved independent material, following the owner protocol's
bounded confirmation policy. An unchanged small repeated panel measures only
repeatability; it does not become unseen qualification. Keep all attempted arms,
errors and overhead; no post-hoc subset or favorable-repeat selection.

Report **retain incumbent**, **prioritize this setting for qualification**, or
**adopt this qualified task/setting** with the quality/economics tradeoff. Record
requested and evidenced settings, actual thinking usage where available, tested
and skipped levels, and whether a genuine within-task level comparison occurred.
A failed low arm supports rejection of low; it does not prove high/max fail.
Still retain the incumbent pending positive qualification. If an untested level
could plausibly reverse the decision, name that missing evidence rather than
claiming the model has been exhausted.
